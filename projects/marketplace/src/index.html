Zogograph Market

Extensions, reviewed deliberately.

Zogograph Market is the public catalog for plugins, collectors, integrations, agent personalities, diagnostic tools, visualization options, communication channels, AI providers, service-specific metrics, and third-party destinations.

What is available here

The catalog and purchase/entitlement service are being separated from the customer-controlled local marketplace. An installed local catalog remains administrator-controlled and does not require a commercial account.

Compatibility

Package IDs stay stable. A public rebrand does not rename installed package records, signing key IDs, update channels, or compatibility declarations.

See the package submission guide for review and signing expectations.

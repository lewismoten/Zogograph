Zogograph Remote

This demonstration represents the small, role-specific counterpart to the full Zogograph client at demo.zogograph.com.

A remote node may collect signals, relay verified ciphertext, or perform another narrowly enrolled role. It does not expose secrets, private host details, unrestricted administrative tools, marketplace management, or the full dashboard.

Endpoints, enrollment, and permitted roles are configured by the operator. Remote collection remains separate from hosted services and is never a reason to send a customer’s recovery material or account database to this demonstration.

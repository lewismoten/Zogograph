A clearer picture of every host.

Observe, record, and explain what is happening across your infrastructure.

Evidence before guesses

Zogograph records signals, history, and trends from the hosts and accounts you choose to observe. Its diagnostics help turn that evidence into useful next steps.

Agents, on your terms

Use built-in agents, configure their instructions and responsibilities, or add agent-related capabilities through plugins. Zogograph does not assume one agent fits every operator or environment.

Extend deliberately

Zogograph Market distributes reviewed plugins and integrations. Hosted services and updates are optional; self-hosted collection remains useful without them.

Availability

The public site describes current self-hosted and demonstration capabilities. Commercial and hosted offerings are introduced only where a service is actually available.
